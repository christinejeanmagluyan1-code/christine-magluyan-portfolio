My journey as an editor began in Grade 11, where a growing interest gradually developed into a passion and a skill I continue to refine. Since then, editing has become both a creative outlet and a meaningful part of my growth. Now in college, I continue to explore new techniques, sharpen my skills, and challenge myself to create work that reflects my creativity, dedication, and evolving style.

I create a variety of content, including AMVs, gaming montages, cinematic edits, social media videos, and photo manipulations. Beyond being a passion, editing has also become a way for me to take on different projects and earn extra income to help cover my school expenses. Through these experiences, I continue to gain valuable skills, build my portfolio, and grow both creatively and professionally.
